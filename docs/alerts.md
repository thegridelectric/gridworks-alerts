# Alert Reference

This document describes each alert generated by `src/gwalert/alert_generator.py` in the **gridworks-alerts** service.

The service runs as a long-lived process (`gwalert`) with a loop every **5 minutes** (`main_loop_seconds`). Each cycle queries the telemetry database for the last **2 hours** (`hours_back`) of `report` and `layout.lite` messages, then runs the checks below.

Alerts are sent to **alert-manager** (`POST /new-alert`) with `message`, `site_alias`, `alert_alias`, and `time_sent` (unix seconds). Alert-manager aliases are `{date}-{site_alias}-{alert_alias}` in `America/New_York` (some alerts append zone or hour suffixes to `alert_alias`).

---

## Data sources

Each cycle loads and parses these message types from the telemetry database:

| Message type | Used for |
|---|---|
| `report` | Channel readings (`channel_reading_list`) and relay states (`state_list`) |
| `layout.lite` | Critical zone list, standby mode (`system_mode`) |
| `glitch` | Critical SCADA glitches (separate query in `check_for_glitches`) |
| `snapshot.spaceheat` | Spruce no-data check only (last **10 minutes**) |

Invalid payloads are skipped with a log line; the rest of the cycle continues.

**Monitored houses** (from report `from_alias`): `beech`, `oak`, `fir`, `maple`, `elm`.

**Standby:** When a `layout.lite` message has `system_mode == "Standby"`, most house-level checks are skipped for that house until a later layout shows a different mode.

**Fetch failures:** If `get_data_from_journaldb()` fails (DB error, no messages, etc.), the cycle logs the error and returns early. Later checks in the same cycle may use **stale data** from the previous successful fetch.

## Active checks (run each cycle)

### 1. Critical Glitches

**Alias:** `critical_glitch`

**What it checks:** `glitch` messages from SCADA systems with `type == "Critical"`.

**Exact trigger:** A parsed glitch has `type == "Critical"`. Each unique `{house_alias}-{summary}` pair alerts once.

**Adjustable parameters:**
- `hours_back` (default: `2`): lookback window for glitch messages.

**What makes it turn off:** Tracked glitches expire after `hours_back` hours; the same house + summary can alert again after expiry.

---

### 2. No Data

**Alias:** `no_data`

**What it checks:** Whether telemetry is still flowing for each monitored house, plus Spruce separately.

**Exact trigger (monitored houses):** Either:
- No channel data was loaded for the house in the current cycle, or
- The most recent channel reading is older than `max_time_no_data` seconds.

**Exact trigger (Spruce):** No `snapshot.spaceheat` messages from `hw1.isone.me.versant.keene.spruce.scada` in the last **10 minutes**, or the newest snapshot is older than `max_time_no_data`.

**Adjustable parameters:**
- `max_time_no_data` (default: `600` s / 10 min)
- `hours_back` (default: `2`): report/layout lookback (Spruce uses a fixed 10-minute window)

**What makes it turn off:** Fresh data arrives within the threshold.

**Note:** Spruce is in `ignored_house_aliases` for report-based checks but is monitored explicitly in this check.

---

### 3. Zone Below Setpoint

**Alias:** `zone_setpoint` (per-zone suffix, e.g. `zone_setpoint_zone1-first`)

**What it checks:** Whether critical zone temperatures have dropped significantly below setpoint.

**Skipped when:** House is in standby; Elm garage zone (temporary fix).

**Exact trigger:** `setpoint - temperature >= max_setpoint_violation_f` °F, and the violation is not explained by a recent setpoint increase (if multiple setpoint values exist in the window).

**Adjustable parameters:**
- `max_setpoint_violation_f` (default: `2` °F)

**What makes it turn off:** Temperature rises to within `max_setpoint_violation_f` of setpoint.

**Notes:**
- When `layout.lite` has been seen for a house (`critical_zones_by_house[house].known`), **only critical zones** are checked.
- When critical zones are not yet known, **all zones** with temp/setpoint channels are checked.
- Zones without setpoint or temperature readings are skipped.

---

### 4. Zone Freezing

**Alias:** `zone_freezing` (per-zone suffix)

**What it checks:** Whether any zone temperature is below a freezing-danger threshold.

**Skipped when:** House is in standby.

**Exact trigger:** Latest zone temperature is below **40 °F**.

**Adjustable parameters:**
- `freezing_threshold`: hardcoded at `40` °F in `check_zone_freezing`.

**What makes it turn off:** Temperature rises to 40 °F or above.

**Note:** Unlike zone setpoint, this checks **all zones** (not only critical zones).

---

### 5. Distribution Pump

**Alias:** `dist_pump`

**What it checks:** Distribution pump flow when there is an active heat call.

**Skipped when:** House is in standby.

**Exact trigger:** All of the following:
1. A zone whitewire heat call has been active for at least **5 minutes** and started at least **5 minutes** ago.
2. No significant `dist-flow` around the heat call (flow ≤ `min_dist_pump_gpm`).
3. This condition is detected on **3 consecutive check cycles** (~15 minutes). The internal counter increments each failing cycle and alerts when it reaches `3`.

**Adjustable parameters:**
- `min_dist_pump_w` (default: `2` W)
- `min_dist_pump_gpm` (default: `0.5` GPM)
- `whitewire_threshold_watts` (default: `20` W; overrides: `beech` `100` W, `elm` `0.9` W)
- Heat-call minimum duration: **5 minutes** (hardcoded)
- Heat call must have started at least **5 minutes** ago (hardcoded)

**What makes it turn off:** `dist-flow` above `min_dist_pump_gpm` around a heat call resets the counter to `0`.

---

### 6. Store Pump

**Alias:** `store_pump`

**What it checks:** Store pump flow when relay 9 is closed.

**Skipped when:** House is in standby; missing `relay9` relay data.

**Exact trigger:**
1. Relay 9 has been `RelayClosed` for more than **10 minutes**.
2. No significant `store-flow` since the relay closed (flow ≤ `min_store_pump_gpm`).

**Adjustable parameters:**
- `min_store_pump_w` (default: `5` W)
- `min_store_pump_gpm` (default: `0.5` GPM)
- Relay closed wait: **10 minutes** (hardcoded)

**What makes it turn off:** Relay 9 opens (`RelayOpen`) or `store-flow` above `min_store_pump_gpm` is detected.

---

### 7. Heat Pump Not Coming On

**Alias:** `hp_on`

**What it checks:** HP power draw when relay states indicate the HP should be running.

**Skipped when:** House is in standby; **Maple is always skipped**; missing `hp-idu-pwr` / `hp-odu-pwr` or `relay5` / `relay6` data.

**Exact trigger:** All of the following:
1. Relay 5 boss state is `Scada` for more than **15 minutes**.
2. Relay 6 boss state is `RelayClosed` for more than **15 minutes**.
3. Neither `hp-odu-pwr` nor `hp-idu-pwr` has drawn ≥ `min_hp_kw` in the last **15 minutes**.

**Adjustable parameters:**
- `min_hp_kw` (default: `1` kW)
- Relay / power wait: **15 minutes** (hardcoded)

**What makes it turn off:** Relay states change so the HP should not be on, or HP power ≥ `min_hp_kw` is seen within 15 minutes.

**Note:** Monoblock systems are not yet handled (`TODO` in code).

---

### 8. HP On During On-Peak

**Alias:** `hp_onpeak` (may append `_{hour}`)

**What it checks:** HP running during on-peak electricity hours.

**Skipped when:** House is in standby.

**Exact trigger:** `hp-odu-pwr` or `hp-idu-pwr` ≥ `min_hp_kw` during an on-peak hour on a weekday (`day_of_week < 5`). A **2-minute grace** applies at the start of the 7 AM and 4 PM hours.

**Adjustable parameters:**
- `min_hp_kw` (default: `1` kW)
- `on_peak_hours` (default: `[7, 8, 9, 10, 11, 16, 17, 18, 19]`)

**What makes it turn off:** No on-peak HP power readings in the current cycle.

---

### 9. Rebooting

**Alias:** `rebooting`

**What it checks:** SCADA rebooting via bursts of `layout.lite` messages.

**Exact trigger:** More than **5** `layout.lite` messages for the same house within the same 5-minute window (within the `hours_back` lookback).

**Adjustable parameters:**
- Reboot threshold: **5** messages per 5-minute window (hardcoded)
- `hours_back` (default: `2`)

**What makes it turn off:** No 5-minute window exceeds the threshold.

---

## Disabled checks (present in code, not called from `main`)

### Not in ATN — `check_in_atn` / `not_in_atn`

Would alert when relay 5 boss is `auto.lc.n.relay5` instead of `ltn.la.relay5`. Commented out in `main()`.

### No More Oil — `check_no_more_oil` / `no_more_oil`

Disabled with a bare `return` at the top of the function. Commented out in `main()`.

---

## Global parameters

| Parameter | Default | Description |
|---|---|---|
| `main_loop_seconds` | `300` (5 min) | Time between check cycles |
| `hours_back` | `2` | Lookback for reports, layout lites, and glitches |
| `ignored_house_aliases` | `['moss', 'orange', 'spruce']` | Excluded from report-based house selection (Spruce monitored separately) |
| `timezone_str` | `America/New_York` | Timezone for time calculations and alert-manager alias dates |
| `SIMULATING` | `False` | When `True`, freezes `reference_now()` for testing |
